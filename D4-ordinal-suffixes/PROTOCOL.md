# D4 confirmation protocol: how small models choose an ordinal suffix

**Status: FROZEN 8 Nov 2026, 04:16 IST** (drafted from 02:47). This file and the scripts under "Frozen code" are read-only, with their SHA-256 in `FROZEN.md`. Later changes are dated amendments, never edits.

Everything below is **prospective**: it is written after exploration (`NOTEBOOK.md`, 7–8 Nov) and before any model reads confirmation material. The confirmation part is run once. The reserve part stays unread.

## 1. What exploration found (candidates, all exploratory)

1. **Token-boundary account (behaviour).** In every qualifying model the teen check ("11th", "112th", not "112nd") works well when the tens digit and the last digit are in the same token, and badly when they are in different tokens of a multi-digit tokeniser. OLMo-2-1B cuts plain numbers into chunks of three from the left, so the tens digit falls in the previous token at 4 and 7 digits but not at 3, 5 or 6; with a thousands separator the last three digits are one token again. Exploration showed low teen AUROC at 4 and 7 digits only, and a repair with the separator. In GPT-2 and Pythia-1B the same split arises only for a few numbers (for example 1012 cut as `101`+`2`); there teen accuracy collapsed. Tokenisers with one token per digit (Qwen3, SmolLM2, Gemma-4) showed a smooth decline with length and no rise and fall.
2. **Early teen copy (mechanism, Qwen3-0.6B-Base).** Layer-0 head L0H2 (with L1H6) attends from the last digit to the tens digit and carries it to the last position; MLP0 and late MLPs (16, 18, 24, 25) turn this into the suffix choice. Patching L0H2's output alone moves a large share of the suffix logit difference for pairs that change teen status (312 vs 352), and nothing for pairs that change only the last digit. Mean-ablating L0H2+L1H6 at the last position removed most of the teen effect at 3 and 4 digits, while random head pairs from layers 0–2 removed nothing; two-digit teens survived.
3. **OLMo-2 cross-token move (mechanism).** For 3-digit numbers (one token) no attention head at the last position matters; the late MLPs (10, 11, 13) read the teen status from the token itself. For 4-digit numbers (`834`+`3`) layer-0 heads L0H5, L0H0 and L0H9 carry the previous token to the last position, and the same late MLPs follow. Mean-ablating these three heads together at the last position removed most of the teen effect at 4 and 7 digits and none at 3 and 5 digits, while random triples from layers 0–2 removed nothing (`outputs/exp05_olmo2-1b_0.5-0.0-0.9.json`).

## 2. Material

- `code/common.py` checks the data digests against `data/SHA256SUMS` before every read.

## 3. Measures

- **m** for an item ending in 1, 2 or 3: logit of the suffix the last digit alone calls for (st, nd, rd) minus the logit of "th", at the number's last token. High m means "treated as a non-teen".
- **Teen AUROC** of a cell: the probability that a non-teen item ending in 1–3 has a higher m than a teen item (x11–x13). 1 = perfect separation, 0.5 = no teen check. It is free of the cell's overall pull towards "th", which raw accuracy is not.
- **Teen effect**: mean m over non-teen items minus mean m over teen items.
- **Accuracy**: the correct suffix is top-1 among st, nd, rd and th.
- **Patch fraction** (exp04): logit difference (source suffix minus target suffix) moved by patching one component's output at the last position from the source run into the target run, summed over pairs whose source and target are both answered correctly, divided by the summed source-minus-target gap over the same pairs.
- **Effect kept** (exp05): teen effect with the listed heads mean-ablated at the last position, divided by the unablated teen effect on the same items.
- 95% intervals for AUROC and accuracy cells in Section 4.1 come from 2,000 bootstrap resamples of distinct numbers (all items of a number move together; seeds `d4-conf-boot-<cell>-<measure>`). **Decisions use the point estimates**; the intervals are reported beside them.

## 4. Predictions

Each prediction passes or fails as written. All are reported, whichever way they go. Thresholds were set from exploration with margin and are not tuned on confirmation data.

### 4.1 Token-boundary account (behaviour; `code/conf01_boundary.py`)

OLMo-2-1B, 200 teen and 200 non-teen numbers ending in 1–3 per length, 3–7 digits, plain form, and for 4–7 digits the same items with a thousands separator ("1,112"):

- **B1 (break at the boundary).** Plain 4-digit and plain 7-digit teen AUROC are each **≤ 0.80**. (Exploration: 0.724 and 0.708.)
- **B2 (no break inside a token).** Plain 3-, 5- and 6-digit and comma 4-, 5-, 6- and 7-digit teen AUROC are each **≥ 0.88**. (Exploration: 0.934–0.990.)
- **B3 (separator repair).** Comma minus plain teen AUROC is **≥ 0.10** at 4 digits and at 7 digits. (Exploration: +0.211 and +0.232.)
- **B4 (rise and fall with length).** AUROC(5 plain) − AUROC(4 plain) **≥ 0.10** and AUROC(6 plain) − AUROC(7 plain) **≥ 0.10**; and plain teen accuracy is **≤ 0.65** at 4 digits while **≥ 0.85** at 3 digits. (Exploration: +0.258, +0.231; 0.435 against 0.950.)

The account is **supported** if B1–B4 all pass, **partly supported** if B1 and B3 pass but B2 or B4 fails, and **not supported** otherwise.

GPT-2 and Pythia-1B (secondary, few independent numbers): the boundary set is every confirmation number from 1,000 to 99,999 ending in 1–3 whose last token in that tokeniser holds a single digit, in every confirmation rankings and positions frame; the control set is 200 teen and 200 non-teen confirmation numbers of 4–5 digits whose last token holds the tens digit.

- **B5.** For each of the two models: boundary teen accuracy **≤ 0.50** while control teen accuracy **≥ 0.85**, and boundary teen AUROC is **at least 0.10 below** control teen AUROC. (Exploration: GPT-2 0.167 vs 0.955, AUROC 0.450 vs 0.947; Pythia-1B 0.321 vs 0.955, AUROC 0.803 vs 0.948.) Reported with the number of distinct teen numbers behind the boundary set (expected 5–10 per model); if a model has fewer than 3, B5 is not read for it.

### 4.2 Early teen copy in Qwen3-0.6B-Base (mechanism)

Ablation (`code/exp05_ablate.py`, heads L0H2 and L1H6, 96 teen and 96 non-teen numbers ending in 1–3 per digit count, 2, 3 and 4 digits, 20 random head pairs from layers 0–2 excluding the candidates):

- **Q1 (necessity, specific).** Ablating L0H2+L1H6 keeps **≤ 0.55** of the teen effect at 3 digits and at 4 digits, and **every** random pair keeps **≥ 0.85** at both. (Exploration: 0.37 and 0.36; random 0.99–1.04.)
- **Q2 (the predicted error).** With L0H2+L1H6 ablated, teen accuracy falls by **≥ 0.20** at 3 and at 4 digits, while non-teen accuracy falls by **≤ 0.10**. (Exploration: −0.573 and −0.302; −0.021 and −0.032.)
- **Q3 (two-digit teens spared; numbers 11–13 reused from exploration).** At 2 digits the ablation keeps **≥ 0.55** of the teen effect and teen accuracy stays **≥ 0.95**. (Exploration: 0.70 and 1.000.)
- **Q4 (L0H2 is the main head).** L0H2 ablated alone keeps **≤ 0.70** at 3 and at 4 digits; L1H6 alone keeps **≥ 0.80**. (Exploration: 0.53, 0.50; 0.93, 0.93.)

Patching (`code/exp04_scan.py`, 96 pairs per scan, head outputs at the last position; `intoteen` pairs change the tens digit into or out of 1 with the target a teen, `last` pairs change only the last digit of a non-teen):

- **Q5 (sufficiency of the copy).** In the 3- and 4-digit `intoteen` scans, L0H2 has the **largest** patch fraction of all heads and that fraction is **≥ 0.40**. (Exploration: 0.63 at 3 digits and 0.65 at 4 digits, top in both.)
- **Q6 (specific to the tens digit).** In the 3- and 4-digit `last` scans, L0H2's patch fraction is **≤ 0.05** in absolute value, while the largest head fraction is **≥ 0.30**. (Exploration: L0H2 −0.000 and −0.000; top head L0H3 0.63 and 0.62.)

The candidate is **supported** if Q1, Q2, Q4 and Q5 all pass; Q3 and Q6 are secondary and reported. **Partly supported** if Q1 and Q2 pass but Q4 or Q5 fails (necessity without the single-head account). **Not supported** if Q1 or Q2 fails.

### 4.3 OLMo-2 cross-token move (mechanism; `code/exp05_ablate.py`)

OLMo-2-1B, heads L0H5, L0H0 and L0H9 mean-ablated together at the last position; 96 teen and 96 non-teen numbers ending in 1–3 per length, plain form, 3, 4, 5 and 7 digits; 20 random triples of heads from layers 0–2 excluding the candidates. The account says these heads matter only where the tens digit is in the previous token (4 and 7 digits), not where it is inside the last token (3 and 5 digits).

- **O1 (necessity across the boundary, specific).** The ablation keeps **≤ 0.65** of the teen effect at 4 digits and at 7 digits, and **every** random triple keeps **≥ 0.85** at both. (Exploration: 0.41 and 0.26; random 0.94–1.02.) Because the unablated teen effect is small at these lengths, O1 is read at a length only if that effect is **≥ 0.30** logits there (exploration: +1.49 and +0.87); if it is smaller at either length, O1 is "not read" and reported as such.
- **O2 (no role inside a token).** The ablation keeps **≥ 0.90** of the teen effect at 3 digits and at 5 digits. (Exploration: 1.00 and 0.99.)

The OLMo-2 account is **supported** if O1 and O2 both pass, **not supported** if either fails, and **undecided** if O1 is not read.

## 5. Runs (once each, confirmation part)

In order, each through `code/run.sh` (GPU lock, memory cage, 20-minute timeout), from `code/chain_conf.sh`:

1. `conf01_boundary.py olmo2-1b --part conf --per 200`
2. `conf01_boundary.py gpt2 --part conf --per 200`
3. `conf01_boundary.py pythia-1b --part conf --per 200`
4. `exp05_ablate.py qwen3-06b --heads 0.2,1.6 --digits 2,3,4 --per 96 --random 20 --pool 0-2 --part conf`
5. `exp04_scan.py qwen3-06b --digits 3 --kind intoteen --n 96 --what head --part conf`, and the same for `--digits 4`, and for `--kind last` at 3 and 4 digits
6. `exp05_ablate.py olmo2-1b --heads 0.5,0.0,0.9 --digits 3,4,5,7 --per 96 --random 20 --pool 0-2 --part conf`

Then `code/conf_evaluate.py` reads the saved outputs and writes `outputs/conf/EVALUATION.json` with every number and each pass or fail. `REPORT.md` quotes only that file, and `code/check_report.py` checks every number in `REPORT.md` against it.

**Failures of the run itself.** A run that crashes or times out (out of memory, lock timeout) is restarted with the same command and seed; this is not a second look, since no output was written. A run that finishes is never repeated. If a script has a bug that changes a number, the fix is a dated amendment, the original output is kept, and both are reported.
